# 0.7.2-beta — first public beta

- Illustrated shoulder, hand and head-turn exercises with progress and completion sounds.
- Eye breaks, movement breaks and configurable water, meal and snack reminders.
- English and Russian interfaces with client-based detection and manual selection.
- Fresh reminder session on each real login; reloads and zone changes preserve countdowns.
- Optional Travelcraft countdown integration, campfire suggestions and timed raid chat breaks.
- Reminders and sounds suppressed throughout battlegrounds and arenas, including out of combat.
- Beta artwork remains in progress. Actual Forever API behavior may vary between beta builds.
